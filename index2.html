About me

Hii all, My name Babul Palei, and I am from Balungaon, Odisha. I got my start as a graphic designer by creating a logo for my dad's barbeque joint. As locals began to see my work, I began designing materials for everything from western wear to crawdads on a stick. Today, I am open to all kinds of design work, although I am always drawn to passion projects that let me go back to my home roots. When I'm not working on my latest graphic design project, I might be thrift shopping, cuddling with my cat or volunteering at my local food shelter.
